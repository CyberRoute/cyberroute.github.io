About me

whoami

Welcome to my blog!!!

Hello, I’m Alessandro, Software Engineer in Security, and passionate musician based in Rome, Italy. With over 15 years of experience in the tech industry, my journey began as a self-taught Linux engineer, only later I got into coding and I liked it a lot…

Driven by a relentless curiosity and a commitment to continuous improvement, I transitioned into roles as a Site Reliability Engineer (SRE) and DevSecOps professional. This journey provided me with invaluable insights into cloud infrastructure, operational practices, applications, databases, global distributed systems and the importance of automation in enhancing security postures.

Throughout my career, I’ve spearheaded global initiatives aimed at fortifying digital ecosystems, from enforcing encryption to pioneering identity automation solutions and securing secrets through the use of different secret managers. My expertise spans a spectrum of Cloud platforms, complemented by a robust skill set in software development methodologies, SDLC, CI/CD practices, and containerization.

In addition to my technical pursuits, I’m committed to advancing the principles of open-source software. I actively contribute to projects that foster collaboration and innovation within the community, recognizing the power of collective knowledge in driving progress. I love the field of Security Research!!!

Beyond the realm of technology, I find inspiration and creativity in music and books, I love the blues, and outdoor sports.

Through insightful articles, projects, and discussions, my aim is to share my experiences and insights, hoping they will be valuable to the readers.

Connect with me on LinkedIn or drop me a line at cyberroute@proton.me. I’m excited to exchange ideas and insights!
